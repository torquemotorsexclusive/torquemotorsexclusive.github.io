How Superbike Imports to Pakistan Actually Work — By Lahore's Import Specialists (2026 Guide)

From finding the right machine abroad to keys in your hand in Lahore — the complete superbike import process for Pakistan, explained by the team at Torque Motorsports, DHA Lahore.

At our showroom in DHA Phase 2, Lahore, we get the same question every week on WhatsApp: "How does importing a superbike to Pakistan actually work?" Most people assume it's either impossibly complicated or suspiciously simple. It's neither. Here's the honest walkthrough from a team that does this every month.

Where the Bikes Come From

We source primarily from Japan, the UK, and the UAE. Japanese auction bikes usually offer the best combination of low mileage and verified history — the auction sheet system there documents everything. UK and European bikes often come with complete dealer service records. UAE bikes have the advantage of proximity and faster shipping to Karachi port.

Every machine is inspected before we commit — either by us directly or by a trusted local inspector. If the history doesn't check out, we walk away. There are always more bikes.

The Cost Structure (What Actually Determines Your Price)

Rather than quoting numbers that change with every exchange rate movement, here's what determines your landed cost in Pakistan:

The bike's price abroad — driven by model, year, mileage, and condition. Shipping and clearing — sea freight to Karachi, port handling, and transport up to Lahore. Government duties — calculated on engine capacity and assessed value, and for bikes above 600cc this is the single largest addition.

Duties change with policy, exchange rates move weekly, and every model is assessed differently. That's why we quote per-bike, current, and real — message us with the model you want and you'll get an actual landed figure for Pakistan today, not a website estimate that's outdated by the time you read it.

The Timeline

From confirmation to keys in your hand in Lahore typically runs 6 to 10 weeks: sourcing and inspection, crating and sea freight, customs clearance at Karachi, transport to Lahore, then registration. Some machines we already have at our Lahore showroom move much faster.

The Part Everyone Underestimates: Paperwork

Export certificate, bill of lading, customs declaration, duty assessment, Punjab excise registration — the documentation chain is navigable but genuinely complex, and it's where first-time importers across Pakistan get stuck for months. We handle the entire chain from Karachi port to Lahore registration. It's most of what you're paying a specialist for.

Condition on Arrival

Bikes travel professionally crated. Most arrive in the same condition they left in; occasionally one needs minor attention after transit. Either way, we tell you exactly which — before handover at our Lahore showroom, not after. We don't hide pre-existing issues, because we want you riding this bike five years from now, not fighting with us in five weeks.

Import vs Buying Locally in Pakistan

Bikes already in the local market — whether in Lahore, Karachi, or Islamabad — carry every cost above plus the seller's margin, and you take their word on history. Importing through a specialist means you choose the exact machine, see its verified history, and know precisely what you're paying for at every step.

We'll tell you whether it's realistic to source, what the current timeline looks like, and give you a real landed-cost estimate at today's rates — no obligation. From Kawasaki Ninja H2Rs to Ducati Panigales, if it exists, we can get it to Pakistan.
