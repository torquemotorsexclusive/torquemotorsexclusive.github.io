The Story.

Two riders. One workshop. A reputation built on bikes nobody else could get into Pakistan.

Torque Motorsports started the way the best motorcycle stories always start: with a kid obsessed with engine noise. Sunny Chaudary grew up convinced that the deep, ragged engine roar of passing van convoys was the sound of superbikes. It wasn't — it was just the vans — but the love stuck. Years later, he imported his first bike, and not long after, became the first man to introduce Ducati to Pakistan.

That alone might've been enough. But Sunny needed a partner with the discipline to turn a passion into a business — and he found it in Saeed Munwar. Saeed brought years of experience importing cars and bikes, restoring them, rebuilding them, and turning rare machines into ready-to-ride realities. Sunny is the heart. Saeed is the precision. Together they built Torque.

Today, we deal in every major motorcycle brand. We don't sell off a lot — we source on demand. You tell us the bike, we find it, we import it, we deliver it. Plus a full workshop in Lahore for anyone, anywhere in Pakistan, who needs their bike serviced, restored, or customized.
